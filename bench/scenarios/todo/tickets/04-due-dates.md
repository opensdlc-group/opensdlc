# Due dates

Change request. Some todos have a deadline, and the list should put them first.

- When I add a todo I can give it a due date, as a calendar date (YYYY-MM-DD). A date that doesn't exist, like 2026-02-30 or "tomorrow", is an error and saves nothing.
- Every todo shows its due date, or that it has none.
- The list shows todos with a due date first, earliest first, then the todos without one. Todos that tie keep the order they were added in.
- Marking a todo done keeps its due date.
